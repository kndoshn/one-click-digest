The maker of the fictional Brightwarm 1500 space heater is recalling about 84,000 units after receiving 37 reports of the heater's power cord overheating, including four reports of minor burns. No fires have been reported.

The recall covers heaters sold between August 2025 and January 2026 at hardware stores and online. The model number, BW-1500C, is printed on a label on the back of the heater.

Owners should stop using the heater and unplug it. The company will send a free replacement cord and a prepaid return label for the old one. Customers can request the kit on the company's website or by phone.
